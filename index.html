FM Synthesis Simulator

Objective: Experiment with frequency ratios and the modulation index in FM synthesis, overcoming a challenge to design a metallic bell sound.

Instructions:

  1. Click Start Simulator.
  2. In FM, one oscillator (the Modulator) changes the speed of another (the Carrier).
  3. Follow the guided missions to hear how integer ratios create musical harmonics (like an Electric Piano).
  4. Complete the final unguided challenge (Mission 5) to create a metallic, inharmonic Bell.
  5. You can use the large button or your Spacebar / Enter key to trigger the sound.
